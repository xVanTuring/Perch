### Fixed
- **Sticky notes can be dragged again** — with "Double-click title to collapse" turned off (the default), a sticky note could not be moved at all. The title strip now always drags the window; the setting only decides whether a double-click collapses the note.
- Clicking quickly through notes in the manager sidebar no longer starts a drag by accident. A drag now begins only after you move the pointer a little and hold the button for a moment.

### Improved
- Dragging a note in the manager sidebar shows the whole row — color bar, title and progress — instead of just the title.
- The manager sidebar updates in place: selection and expanded groups stay as they are, and a note moved to another group slides into its new place.
- The task progress pie stays visible on the selected row in the sidebar.

### 修复
- **便签又能拖动了** —— 关闭「双击标题折叠」（默认就是关闭的）时，便签窗口完全拖不动。现在按住顶部标题条始终可以拖动窗口，这个开关只决定双击时是否折叠。
- 在管理窗口侧栏里快速点选笔记时，不会再被误当成拖动。现在要稍微移动鼠标并按住一小会儿才会开始拖动。

### 改进
- 在管理窗口侧栏拖动笔记时，跟随鼠标的是整行内容（颜色条、标题和进度），不再只有标题文字。
- 管理窗口侧栏改为原地更新：选中项和展开的分组保持不变，笔记移到别的分组时会以动画移到新位置。
- 侧栏里选中的那一行，任务进度圆饼也能看清了。
